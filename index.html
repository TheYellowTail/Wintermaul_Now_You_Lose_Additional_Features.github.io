At level 15 players choose a second race and then a third race at level 30

With the third and second race that players choose they will choose races that allow them to make sure they have attack type counters to every armor type. As the end game requires coverage for all armor types.|

-Fortify Mechanic

Players can type -fortify at any time in the game. This command gives bonus gold but can only be used once. The amount of gold is increased the later the level is. So you will use this strategically, only using it when you really need it and using it in the later levels when it will give more gold.

Splash and Area of Effect towers deal the most damage but can only be used when enemies are clumped. Enemies clump together at the spawn area for a few seconds allowing Splash towers to deal damage.

Single Target Towers would be used to finish enemies after off after they are no longer clumped. Single target towers can kill enemies effectively in your tower maze
Most Ultimate Towers are splash damage towers and spell damage towers are the strongest. This version demands the usage of splash damage towers because enemies get strong and the additional damage splash towers do over single target towers is required
The lives you lose in the early game transfer over to how far you will make it into the late game Level 13 Unstoppable Abominations use the Warcraft 3 Abomination death sound effect. Level 35 Vengeful Champions use the Illidan death sound effect. Level 36 Unstoppable Hellfire Infernals use the Infernal death sound effect. Level 35 and level 36 death sounds hint toward the death sounds of the abominations at level 13, giving players flashbacks both in the future and in the past. Level 25 Mantorok The Dead god uses the Warcraft 3 C'thun model and has slow movement speed. It is like a final boss except placed in the middle of the game instead of at the end. The term 'dead god' refers that this may have been an ancient cult/civilization. With level 39 Twilight Minister hinting the word 'Minister' as if he was the leader of the cult for for Mantorok the Dead God.

The way the laser cannon is the only way to kill Mantorok at level 25 is like a hint that this is a god that can only be slain using a sacred weapon
